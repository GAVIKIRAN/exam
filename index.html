Before you start

How to be ready

  1. Be seated and logged in with at least 10 minutes to spare — connection or login issues eat into your writing time, not the other way around.
  2. Close every other tab and app. Keep only what the exam itself needs open.
  3. Have your ID, admit card, and any permitted materials on the desk before the window opens.
  4. Do a quick check: charger plugged in, webcam working if required, room quiet.
  5. Once the window opens, confirm the on-screen timer matches what you expect before you begin answering.
  6. Submit with a few minutes of buffer left — don't wait for an auto-submit to do it for you.

Common questions

What if I lose connection mid-exam?
Reconnect and log back in immediately — most platforms resume where your session left off, but don't count on extra time being granted.
Can I leave and come back?
Treat the window as one continuous sitting unless your exam's own instructions say otherwise.
Does this page submit anything for me?
No — this is only a timer and checklist. You still take the exam on its own platform.
